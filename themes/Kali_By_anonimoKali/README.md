### ✨ Highlights

* **Perfect Contrast:** True black backgrounds designed to save battery and completely prevent glare.

### 📱 Supported Versions (Included in ZIP):
- **105px:** M5StickC Plus / Plus 2
- **140px:** T-Embed CC1101 / Plus
- **180px:** CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) & T-Deck
- **192px:** T-LoRa-Pager

### 📦 What's Inside the ZIP?
- Pre-configured folders ready for drag-and-drop.
- `.json` configuration files for both **Kali** and **Kali Border** setups.
- Dedicated monochrome assets tailored for each resolution.

### 🚀 Quick Installation
1. Download the `Kali_Themes_By_anonimoKali.zip` below.
2. Extract the archive on your computer.
3. Drag the folders into the `/themes` directory on your device's SD card.
4. Go to **Settings > Themes** on your Bruce menu, and pick your favorite version!
