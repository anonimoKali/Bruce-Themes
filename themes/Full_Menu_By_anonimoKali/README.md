Introducing **Full Menu**, a total overhaul of the standard interface navigation designed to maximize efficiency and screen real estate on your **Bruce Firmware** device. 

This theme drops standard centered templates in favor of an advanced **dual-panel layout**, giving you an immediate overview of both your active wireless module and its specific sub-menu capabilities simultaneously.

---

### 🎨 Design & Visual Highlights:

* **Dual-Panel Navigation:** The left side handles the main operational modes (WiFi, BLE, ETH, RF) with crisp, clear iconography. The right side features a smooth, rounded solid block hosting your attack/tool selection.
* **Streamlined UI Workflow:** No more guessing where you are. The persistent sidebar highlights your current module while you scroll through lists.
* **Premium High-Contrast Finish:** Perfectly tuned text tracking and custom status bar icons (Battery, SD, Status) built for quick glance verification during field operations.

---

### 📱 Supported Versions (Included in ZIP):
- **105px:** M5StickC Plus / Plus 2
- **140px:** T-Embed CC1101 / Plus
- **180px:** CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) & T-Deck
- **192px:** T-LoRa-Pager

### 📦 What's Inside the ZIP?
- Pre-configured directory architecture ready for drag-and-drop.
- Master `.json` configuration files precision-mapped for each screen resolution.
- Exclusive asset folders containing the new custom split-screen panel elements.

### 🚀 Quick Installation
1. Download the `Full_Menu_By_anonimoKali.zip` asset below.
2. Extract the folder contents onto your PC.
3. Drag and drop the extracted folders into the `/themes` directory of your device's SD card.
4. Reboot your device, navigate to **Settings > Themes**, and deploy **Full Menu**!
