The legendary terminal-style layout is back, now in a high-visibility, razor-sharp white aesthetic. **Total Tools White** is designed for users who want the ultimate tactical look without sacrificing readability.

Featuring clean geometric indicators, sharp framing, and a custom-crafted WiFi status indicator, this theme turns your **Bruce Firmware** menus into a high-tech control deck.

---

### 🎨 Features & Visual Highlights:

* **High-Contrast Terminal Design:** Pure white assets on a deep black background for battery-saving performance and zero outdoor glare.
* **Dual-Style Menus:** Beautifully optimized for both primary list menus (with custom slanted selectors) and advanced sub-menus enclosed in futuristic bracket frames.
* **Tactical Nav Arrows:** Custom sharp navigation indicators that make cycling through attacks, sniffers, and portals feel incredibly responsive.

---

### 📱 Supported Versions (Included in ZIP):
- **105px:** M5StickC Plus / Plus 2
- **140px:** T-Embed CC1101 / Plus
- **180px:** CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) & T-Deck
- **192px:** T-LoRa-Pager

### 📦 What's Inside the ZIP?
- Pre-configured folders ready for drag-and-drop installation.
- `.json` configuration files tailored for each specific hardware resolution.
- High-definition, custom-pixelated white terminal icons.

### 🚀 Quick Installation
1. Download the `Total_Tools_White_By_anonimoKali.zip` archive below.
2. Extract the files on your computer.
3. Copy the folders into the `/themes` directory of your device's SD card.
4. Open your Bruce menu, head to **Settings > Themes**, and load **Total Tools White**!
