**Guillotine White** is the newest addition to the collection, designed for those who prefer a "razor-sharp" user interface. This theme strips away all visual noise, focusing on high-contrast lines and a vertical, blade-like precision that makes navigating your **Bruce Firmware** menus feel instantaneous and clean.

It’s not just a theme; it’s a tool for speed. The "Guillotine" aesthetic is defined by its clean, intersecting lines and stark white-on-black clarity, ensuring perfect legibility under any condition.

### ✨ Highlights
* **Razor-Sharp UI:** Intersecting lines and a "slicing" layout that gives the interface a distinct, aggressive look.
* **Extreme Contrast:** Optimized for maximum visibility and zero eye fatigue.
* **Minimalist Architecture:** Designed for users who want to focus purely on their tools and data without graphical distractions.
* **Precision Alignment:** Every element is locked to a sharp, high-definition grid.

### 📱 Supported Versions (Included in ZIP):
- **105px:** M5StickC Plus / Plus 2
- **140px:** T-Embed CC1101 / Plus
- **180px:** CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) & T-Deck
- **192px:** T-LoRa-Pager

### 📦 What's Inside?
- Ready-to-deploy folder structure.
- `Guillotine_White.json` configuration files for all supported resolutions.
- High-contrast, sharp-edged monochrome assets.

### 🚀 Installation
1. Download `Guillotine_White_By_anonimoKali.zip` below.
2. Extract the files to your computer.
3. Move the folders into the `/themes` directory on your SD card.
4. Set **Guillotine_White** as your theme in **Settings > Themes**.
