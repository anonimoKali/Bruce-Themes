Take control of your device with the new **World Theme**, designed to turn your **Bruce Firmware** interface into a global operations terminal. 

Featuring a beautifully detailed, high-contrast digital world map with network nodes, this theme brings a professional cyber-tactical aesthetic to your handheld. It's designed to look like a global monitoring system, making it one of the most immersive themes in the collection.

---

### ✨ Highlights
* **Global Network Aesthetic:** A stunning pixelated world map with glowing link pathways.
* **Futuristic HUD Corner Brackets:** Framed perfectly to give it a military-grade console feel.
* **Tactical Green Optimization:** Designed to look phenomenal on devices with green LEDs (like the T-Embed) but perfectly clear on any display.
* **Ultra-Readable Fonts:** High-contrast text labels for quick navigation.

### 📱 Supported Versions (Included in ZIP):
- **105px:** M5StickC Plus / Plus 2
- **140px:** T-Embed CC1101 / Plus
- **180px:** CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) & T-Deck
- **192px:** T-LoRa-Pager

### 📦 What's Inside the ZIP?
- Pre-configured folders ready for drag-and-drop.
- `.json` configuration files optimized for all supported resolutions.
- All dedicated pixel-art assets.

### 🚀 Quick Installation
1. Download the `World_By_anonimoKali.zip` below.
2. Extract the archive on your computer.
3. Drag the folders into the `/themes` directory on your device's SD card.
4. Go to **Settings > Themes** on your Bruce menu and select **World**!
