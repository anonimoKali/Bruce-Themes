> [!IMPORTANT]
> These animations are **tailored for M5Stack hardware**. They are optimized for the vertical/horizontal aspect ratios and screen refresh rates specific to these controllers. Using them on different hardware might result in cropped or distorted images.

## 🚀 How to Install

To set a custom boot animation on your M5Stack device, follow these steps:

1.  **Download:** Select your preferred `.gif` file from this folder (ensure it matches your screen orientation) and save it to your computer.
2.  **Access Storage:** Connect your device or its SD card to your PC.
3.  **Transfer:** Copy the `.gif` file to the **root directory** (the main folder) of your SD card or LittleFS partition.
4.  **Rename:** You **must** rename the file to exactly `boot.gif` for the Bruce Firmware to recognize it during the boot sequence.
5.  **Apply:** Perform a hard reset or power cycle your device. The new animation will play immediately upon startup.

---
*Tip: If the animation feels slow or doesn't appear, ensure you are using a high-speed SD card and that your Bruce Firmware is updated to the latest release.*
