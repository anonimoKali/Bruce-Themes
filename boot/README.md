## 🚀 How to Install

Installing your new boot animation is quick and easy. Follow these steps:

1.  **Download:** Browse this folder, choose your favorite `.gif` file, and download it to your computer.
2.  **Access Storage:** Connect your SD card to your PC (or access the device storage via your firmware's file management tools).
3.  **Transfer:** Copy the downloaded `.gif` file directly into the **root directory** of your SD card or the `LittleFS` partition.
4.  **Rename (Crucial):** Ensure the file is named correctly based on your specific firmware requirements (most systems require it to be named exactly `boot.gif`).
5.  **Reboot:** Power off your device and turn it back on. Your new custom animation should now appear during the boot sequence!

> [!NOTE]
> Ensure your firmware version is up to date and supports custom boot animations.
> If the animation does not appear, verify the filename and ensure it is placed in the root folder, not inside a subfolder.
